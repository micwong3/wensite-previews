Mark V. Ferrante

Trial lawyer · Chicago · Licensed in Illinois

Before founding the firm in April 1991, Mark worked with some of Chicago's top trial lawyers. He has obtained multiple multi-million dollar settlements — one exceeding $12.5 million — and has litigated or settled matters in Illinois and across numerous other states and Canada.

He is an active member of the Illinois Trial Lawyers Association, the American Association for Justice, and the Chicago Bar Association, and has been recognized for excellence in pro bono service by the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois.
